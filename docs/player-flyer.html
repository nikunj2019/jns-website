Live scoring, GPS course map and leaderboard — on your phone

Everything runs in your web browser. There is no app to download, no account to create and no password to remember. Your captain has a private link for your foursome — open it once and your phone remembers your team for the rest of the day.

Getting started

  1. Open your team's link

    Your captain will text it to you. It looks like jnssolutions.ai/golf/?code=CEDAR-EAGLE-472 — the code is what signs you in, so there is nothing else to enter.

  2. Add it to your home screen

    iPhone: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app. It then opens like any other app, full screen and without the browser bars.

  3. Do this before you leave the house

    Phone signal at the course is patchy. Opening the app once on good wifi means the course map and scorecard are already stored on your phone when you get there.

  4. Check you're on the right team

    Tap My team from the ⋯ menu. You should see your foursome and your starting hole — the outing is a shotgun start, so most teams do not begin on hole 1.

Our team code: ______________________
One code per foursome. All four of you use the same one, and all four can enter scores — it's a scramble, so the team records a single score per hole. If two of you enter the same hole, the most recent entry is the one that sticks.

Good to know

One phone is enough

You don't all need it open. One person keeping the card works fine — and if two of you do have it open, you'll both see the same running total.

Watch the battery

The GPS distance reading is the hungry part. Leave it off unless you're using it, and start the round with a full charge.

Nothing to sign up for

No account to make, no password, no email address — the team code is the whole login. Lose your phone and you lose nothing: open the link on another one.

Using the app

Four buttons along the bottom — that's the whole thing

What each screen does

Score

Tap + or − to set your team's score for the hole, then use the arrows to move between holes. It saves by itself — there is no save button. The line underneath tells you where it stands: Saved, or Queued if you have no signal.

Course

An aerial map of every hole with the playing line, distances between points, and par and stroke index. Tap the GPS button top-right to see your live distance to the middle of the green.

Leaderboard

Live standings for every team, updating on their own. Switch between overall, front nine and back nine.

Messages

Announcements from the organizers, and a direct line to ask them anything. Find it via the speech-bubble icon at the top right.

Out of signal

The course has dead spots, and the app is built for them. Scores you enter without signal are held on your phone and sent automatically when you get a bar back — you do not need to re-enter anything, and you can keep scoring holes in the meantime. The badge says Queued until it goes through. The course map works offline too.

If something goes wrong

I lost the link Go to jnssolutions.ai/golf/ and enter your team code by hand. Ask your captain or an organizer if you don't have it.
It says my code isn't found Check it against the card — 0 and O are easy to mix up. If it still fails, an organizer may have reissued it; ask for the current one.
I'm on the wrong team Open your own team's link again. The phone switches to whichever code it was last given.
A score is wrong Go back to that hole and change it — you can edit any hole at any time. Organizers can also correct it from the scoring table.
Distances look off GPS needs permission and a clear sky. It measures to the centre of the green, not the pin, so treat it as a guide.
Nothing is loading You're probably in a dead spot. The leaderboard needs signal; scoring does not.
Something urgent? Messages in the app are read when an organizer is at the scoring table, which is not all the time. For anything that can't wait — an injury, a lost cart, a hold-up on the course — call the clubhouse on (765) 482-7272 or find an organizer.
