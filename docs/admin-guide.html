Signing in

Go to jnssolutions.ai/golf/admin/ and tap Continue with Google, using the Google account your outing address is on. There is no password to remember, and Google accounts arrive already verified, which is what the security rules require.

A password option exists behind Use a password instead for anyone without a Google account. If that address hasn't been verified, the screen offers to send the verification link — writes are refused until it's clicked.

If Google sign-in is refused with "this domain isn't on the Firebase authorized domains list", the domain you're on needs adding in Firebase Console → Authentication → Settings → Authorized domains. Preview links always fail this way; the live site should not.

The five tabs

TabWhat it's for
DashboardLive status — scores entered, teams actively scoring, completion, and per-team progress.
TeamsCreate foursomes, share their private links, edit rosters, reissue codes.
ScoringCorrect or enter any team's score, hole by hole.
MessagesAnnouncements to everyone or selected teams, plus a private thread with each team.
AccessAdd and remove other organizers. Owners only.

Setting up teams

  1. Teams → + New team. Enter the team name, the starting hole for the shotgun, and up to four players. The first player is treated as the captain.
  2. The code is created for you. It appears on the card immediately, in the form CEDAR-EAGLE-472 — two words and three digits, built to be read aloud without being misheard. You never have to generate one by hand.
  3. Send it. Share link opens your phone's share sheet with the message already written, so you can send it by Messages, Mail or WhatsApp in one tap. Copy, Email and Text are there for a laptop.

The other buttons on a team card

Edit rosterChange the name, starting hole or players. The code is unaffected.
New codeIssues a fresh code and kills the old link immediately. Only use this if a code has gone somewhere it shouldn't — anyone already using the old link will be locked out until you re-send.
DeleteRemoves the team, its players and all its scores. Asks first, and cannot be undone.
One code per foursome, shared by all four. Any of them can enter scores. A code only ever reaches that team's card — nobody can score for another team, whatever they do with the link.

During the round

Scoring corrections, announcements and support

2 / 3

Fixing a score

Scoring → pick the team → tap the hole in the grid → adjust with − and +. It saves immediately; there's no confirm step. Holes already scored are shaded, so a team's gaps are visible at a glance.

Worth having someone at the scoring table regardless — a flat phone or a team that never opened the link is the usual reason a card is missing.

What happens when two team-mates score at once

  • Different holes — both are kept. Each hole is written on its own, so the captain entering the 7th and someone catching up the 5th don't overwrite each other.
  • Same hole — the last entry wins. Usually right, since the second person is normally correcting the first.
  • Every phone catches up within a few seconds. Your corrections behave the same way and override whatever the team entered.

Announcements

Messages → write a heading and a message → choose All teams or Pick teams → send.

All teamsPosted publicly. It takes over every player's screen the moment they see it, and stays until they tap "Got it". Afterwards it stays on their Messages screen.
Pick teamsDelivered privately into just those teams' threads. Other teams never see it.

Posted announcements are listed underneath the composer with a Remove button — take one down when it stops being true, since "rain delay" reads badly an hour after play resumed.

Nobody's phone buzzes. There are no push notifications. Messages and announcements appear when a player has the app open, and are waiting with an unread badge when they next open it. For anything that genuinely can't wait, use the loudspeaker or the phone. Players are told the same thing.

Support threads

Each team has a private thread with the organizers. The Messages tab shows every team, including those who've never written, so you can start a conversation with any of them.

  • A red dot on the Messages tab means at least one team is waiting on a reply — it's visible from any tab, so you don't have to keep checking.
  • Teams waiting are highlighted in the list. Opening a thread clears it.
  • Threads are private to that team. No team can see another's, and a player cannot send a message that appears to come from an organizer.

What players see that you don't

Three behaviours on the player side account for most of the questions you'll be asked. None of them is a fault.

  • Scores entered without signal say Queued. They sit on the phone and send themselves when a bar comes back. A card that looks behind on your Dashboard is more often a phone in a dead spot than a team that has stopped scoring — give it until they reach higher ground before chasing them.
  • A JNS card appears after every third hole they complete. It's the sponsor slot. It clears itself after seven seconds, has an × if they're impatient, and never interrupts a score mid-entry — it waits until the hole is done.
  • The app holds a Stonegate splash for about three seconds on every open. That is deliberate, not a slow load.

Adding another organizer

Access → enter their email → choose Administrator or Scorekeeper → Grant access. They then sign in at /golf/admin/ with Google using that exact address.

  • Nothing is emailed automatically — send them the link yourself.
  • The address must match their Google account exactly.
  • Only owners see the Access tab. Owners are fixed in the security rules and cannot be changed from inside the app — which is the point of them.

A running order for the day

WhenDo this
Week beforeCreate every team with its starting hole. Send each captain their link and ask them to open it once and add it to their home screen.
Morning ofCheck the Dashboard shows the right number of teams. Post a welcome announcement — it doubles as a test that announcements reach phones.
At the shotgunNothing to switch on. Scoring is open as soon as teams have their links.
DuringWatch the Dashboard for a team stuck on zero — usually someone who never opened their link. Keep an eye on the Messages dot.
AfterLeaderboard is final as soon as the last card is in. Fix any disputes from the Scoring tab.

Troubleshooting

A team can't get inConfirm they have the current code — if anyone pressed New code, older links stopped working. Re-send from Share link.
A team's card is emptyThey probably never opened the link. Enter their scores from the Scoring tab and sort the phone out afterwards.
A code reads "Not generated"Its code didn't get created. Press Generate code on that team.
Leaderboard won't loadSignal at the scoring table. The screen will say what actually failed rather than guessing — read the message before assuming.
"Access not assigned"That address isn't on the organizer list, or it's a different Google account from the one added. Check the exact spelling in the Access tab.
Wrong scores appearingAny team-mate can score. Correct it from the Scoring tab; your entry wins.
Two things that surprise people. There are no push notifications — anything urgent needs a phone call. And a shared code means any of the four can score, so if a card looks wrong, it is more often a team-mate than a bug.
